## 🤝 Contribute

Found a broken link, a new program, or an outdated rate? You can:

1. **Open a PR** — fork, edit, submit. New entries are merged within days.
2. **Open an issue** — even a "this link is dead" comment helps.
3. **[Tweet / DM me](https://twitter.com/WanyoikeRoy)** — for technical talks I can also share or help get a speaker for your event.

When adding a program, please include: **name · application link · pay rate · topics · last-verified date**.

For non-technical writing sites, check [YoungShark Technologies' technical writing programme](http://www.youngsharktechnologies.com/technical-writing-programme).
